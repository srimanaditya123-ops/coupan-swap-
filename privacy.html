Privacy Policy

This page is a starter privacy-policy template for the CouponSwap project. Before a public commercial launch, it should be reviewed and adapted by a qualified legal professional.

Information

A production version may collect account details, listings, transaction information, support requests and technical information needed to operate the service.

Use

Information would be used to provide accounts, marketplace features, fraud prevention, customer support and required communications.

Payments

Payment information should be handled by a trusted payment provider rather than stored directly by CouponSwap unless appropriate security and compliance controls are implemented.
