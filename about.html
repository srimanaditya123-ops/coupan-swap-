About CouponSwap

CouponSwap is designed as a community marketplace where people can discover, sell, and swap unused coupons, vouchers, and discount codes.

Our idea

Instead of letting a useful coupon expire unused, CouponSwap gives it a second life by connecting the person who has it with someone who wants the saving.

Our model

The planned marketplace can support a 10% platform commission on completed transactions, with seller and buyer flows, coupon verification, ratings, notifications, and secure payments.
