Terms of Use

This is a starter terms template for the CouponSwap project and is not legal advice.

Marketplace use

Users should only list coupons they are legally allowed to transfer and must provide accurate information about value, expiry, restrictions and redemption conditions.

Transactions

A production platform should define payment, cancellation, dispute, refund, verification and commission rules before enabling real transactions.

Prohibited activity

Fraud, counterfeit coupons, misleading listings, unauthorized account access and other unlawful activity should be prohibited.
